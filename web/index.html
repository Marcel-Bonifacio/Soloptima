Balances and main results

GlobHor and DiffHor: global and diffuse horizontal irradiation. GlobInc: irradiation on the module plane before shading; GlobEff: after row shading, reflection (IAM) and soiling. EArray: DC energy of the array after mismatch, wiring, LID and quality; E_avail: energy available after the inverter, clipping, AC losses and availability. PR = E_avail ÷ (GlobInc × kWp ÷ 1 kW/m²). Normalised production per kWp and day: the reference yield Yr (GlobInc) splits into the final yield Yf (E_avail), the system losses Ls (EArray − E_avail) and the array capture losses Lc (GlobInc − EArray). After a simulation, Lu is the PV energy curtailed because neither the load nor the battery could take it, and PR delivered counts only the PV energy used.

Size & operation

Ageing

State of health falls each year by calendar fade + (DC equivalent full cycles × (1 − EOL) / (cycle life × its depth of discharge)). DC cycles = energy discharged ÷ one-way efficiency ÷ nameplate energy. The battery is replaced when it falls to the replacement threshold (blank = end of life) and restarts at 100 %.

Auxiliary loads

Served every hour like the site load, also at night: battery part = nameplate MWh × kW/MWh × (1 + coefficient × ambient above the reference) + PCS MW × kW/MW, with a battery; plant auxiliaries with PV, wind or a battery; PV transformer no-load with PV, BESS transformer no-load with a battery. They do not grow with the load. LCOE, COE and billed energy count only the energy served to the site.

Diesel fleet & control strategy

The genset fleet is fixed (known capacity). The control strategy decides how PV, battery and gensets share the load each hour.

Equipment datasheets

Datasheet library for PV modules, inverters, batteries, diesel generators, transformers and cables. Pick the equipment used in this project ("Use"), then apply it to the model. Built-in entries are generic typical values; import or enter the manufacturer datasheets for design work.

Electrical design & single-line diagram

String sizing, transformer selection and cable sizing (current rating, voltage drop, I²R losses) from the datasheets. Annual losses are integrated over the hourly PV, battery, genset and load profiles and can be fed back into the energy model.

Script console

A numeric workspace on top of the engine: vectors, statistics, curve fitting, plots, tables, parametric sweeps and Monte Carlo runs, with full access to the project and every result. Scripts are JavaScript with numeric helper functions (type help()).

    Energy flow (year 1)

    Where the energy of year 1 comes from (left) and where it ends (right), through the battery and the load, in MWh. Battery losses = energy charged − energy discharged − self-discharge − change in stored energy, so the battery's own balance closes. Unmet is the part of the load not served.

    Operating options

    Simulates the project as it is set now, changing one setting per row: diesel only as the reference, no battery (PV only), every dispatch strategy with the battery, and the predictive EMS with perfect forecasts as a benchmark for better forecasting. Feasible means the optimiser constraints (Optimise sizing) are met. The predictive EMS takes about a second per full-life run.

    Net present cost from diesel only to the hybrid with its best strategy, one step at a time. The split depends on the order: PV is added first, then the battery, then the best strategy. Added before PV, the battery would take part of the saving shown here for PV.

    Units online duration curve

    Gensets online in each hour of year 1, sorted from most to fewest. Firm capacity is set by the peak load and the reserve rule, not by PV. The gap between the curves is daytime unit-hours freed for maintenance, not units that can be retired: forced outages and N-1 are not modelled.

    Battery state of charge by day and hour

    Battery health

    State of health at each year boundary, with the replacement threshold and the replacements (left), and the capacity lost each year to calendar and cycle fade (right). Cycles per year count AC discharge ÷ nameplate energy (check the counting basis of the warranty); the DC value (÷ one-way efficiency) drives the cycle fade. Expected life of a new battery = (100 % − threshold) ÷ year-1 fade.

    What drives the result (tornado)

    Simulates the design as it is set now once at the low and once at the high value of each input below, one input at a time, and shows how far the result moves from its base value. Unlike the sensitivity cases of Optimise sizing, the sizes stay fixed.

    Bars start at the base value: blue for the low input value, amber for the high one. Cycle life and calendar fade move the battery replacement year in whole-year steps, so their bars can be lumpy and asymmetric.

    Optimise sizing

    Searches PV and battery sizes (and wind turbines with the metaheuristics) for the given load and diesel fleet. Each candidate is a full chronological simulation over the project life, or of year 1 only in fast mode.

    Algorithm

    Grid: every point simulated, then refined. PSO and SHO–QAO–GOA: population metaheuristics with constraint penalties. MOPSO: Pareto set of cost vs renewable share (or SSSI); the "best" is the knee point.
