Inter-annual variability and P50/P90

Simulates year 1 of the plant once with each historical weather year, sizes and settings unchanged, and gives P50, P75, P90 and P99 of the specific yield, fuel saved, renewable fraction, revenue and fuel used. Fuel saved and revenue vary less than the PV yield when gensets run at minimum loading, the battery is full or a take-or-pay floor applies, so each result gets its own spread.

The spread is used only as ratios: P50 stays on the project's weather above. The systematic uncertainty (root-sum-square of the three inputs) is scaled by each result's sensitivity to irradiance, found by running the project's weather with its irradiance raised and lowered by that amount. With wind turbines, each weather year also brings its own wind speeds, but the systematic uncertainty and the elasticity cover irradiance only, so the P90 leaves out the uncertainty of the long-term wind resource. PVGIS serves ERA5 reanalysis over most of Indonesia, so cross-check it against NASA POWER. The years are downloaded once per session (PVGIS about a minute, NASA POWER about 20 s per three years) and are not saved in the project.

Balances and main results

GlobHor and DiffHor: global and diffuse horizontal irradiation. GlobInc: irradiation on the module plane before shading; GlobEff: after row shading, reflection (IAM) and soiling. EArray: DC energy of the array after mismatch, wiring, LID and quality; E_avail: energy available after the inverter, clipping, AC losses and availability. PR = E_avail ÷ (GlobInc × kWp ÷ 1 kW/m²). Normalised production per kWp and day: the reference yield Yr (GlobInc) splits into the final yield Yf (E_avail), the system losses Ls (EArray − E_avail) and the array capture losses Lc (GlobInc − EArray). After a simulation, Lu is the PV energy curtailed because neither the load nor the battery could take it, and PR delivered counts only the PV energy used.

Size & operation

Ageing

State of health falls each year by calendar fade + (DC equivalent full cycles × (1 − EOL) / (cycle life × its depth of discharge)). DC cycles = energy discharged ÷ one-way efficiency ÷ nameplate energy. The battery is replaced when it falls to the replacement threshold (blank = end of life) and restarts at 100 %.

Auxiliary loads

Served every hour like the site load, also at night: battery part = nameplate MWh × kW/MWh × (1 + coefficient × ambient above the reference) + PCS MW × kW/MW, with a battery; plant auxiliaries with PV, wind or a battery; PV transformer no-load with PV, BESS transformer no-load with a battery. They do not grow with the load. LCOE, COE and billed energy count only the energy served to the site.

Diesel fleet & control strategy

The genset fleet is fixed (known capacity). The control strategy decides how PV, battery and gensets share the load each hour.

    Reliability, self-sufficiency, environment & social indicators

    How these indicators are defined
    LPSP
    Unmet energy ÷ load.
    REP
    100 × (1 − LPSP).
    SSCI
    Σmin(load, RE) ÷ ΣRE: the share of PV and wind used directly.
    SSSI
    Σmin(load, RE) ÷ Σload: the share of the load met directly by PV and wind.
    HDI
    From electricity per person: 0.0978 ln(E/person) − 0.0319.
    TAC
    NPC × CRF.
    COE
    TAC ÷ energy served to the site (auxiliary consumption excluded, as in the LCOE).
    Loss of load
    Hours with more than 0.1 % of the load unserved. An event is a run of such hours.
    Reserve shortfall
    Hours in which the genset headroom and the battery reserve left after dispatch do not cover the spinning-reserve rule. Normally zero with enough units for the peak load and its reserve; it matters for questions such as one unit fewer for maintenance, a few large units or load growth.
    Low-load running
    Hours with every online genset at minimum loading (wet-stacking risk).

    Optimise sizing

    Searches PV and battery sizes (and wind turbines with the metaheuristics) for the given load and diesel fleet. Each candidate is a full chronological simulation over the project life, or of year 1 only in fast mode.

    Algorithm

    Grid: every point simulated, then refined. PSO and SHO–QAO–GOA: population metaheuristics with constraint penalties. MOPSO: Pareto set of cost vs renewable share (or SSSI); the "best" is the knee point.

    Forecast & energy management

    How well PV and load can be forecast at this site, how the predictive EMS plans the battery and gensets, and an operational day-ahead schedule from a live weather forecast.

    Forecast Lab: compare forecasting methods

    Every method except "Perfect forecast" and "Perfect + random error" uses only data from before the forecast is issued. Errors are normalised by the mean actual value (PV: daylight hours). Skill = 1 − RMSE / RMSE of persistence. "Value" re-runs the predictive EMS for year 1 with each method and compares fuel, genset hours and cost against load following.
